José Rizal: Life & Works

About Our Project

A student-created educational website dedicated to exploring the life, works, experiences, and legacy of Dr. José Rizal.

Our Purpose

Making History Meaningful

Learning about the past helps us better understand the present and our responsibilities toward the future.

The José Rizal: Life & Works website was created to present the life and contributions of Dr. José Rizal in an organized and accessible format. It explores important stages of his life, including his childhood, education, travels, literary works, exile, and final journey.

Through historical information and supporting references, the project aims to help students and readers appreciate Rizal's ideas, his dedication to learning, and his role in Philippine history.

This website is a collaborative academic project. The team worked to organize the content and create a design that makes the material easier to navigate and understand.

Personal Reflection

Learning about the events of José Rizal’s life before he was killed was indeed an eye-opener for me, as I understood the sacrifices that he made for the country. Although Rizal believed that the time was ripe for revolution, he believed that an armed uprising should not begin without enough preparation and support. It was also fascinating to see his journey as a doctor and a patriot who did not abandon his duty as a physician even when his country was in dire need of a leader. Still, Rizal did not fail to disappoint as the events surrounding his arrest and the unfair trial that led to his execution revealed the injustice that he was subjected to by the Spanish authorities.

Perhaps the most intriguing part was observing the life of a patriot who had accepted death as a fate but continued to express his love for the Philippines, as evident in his poem Mi Último Adiós. Rizal, who realized that his time had come, made sure to signify the importance of freedom in his countrymen’s lives, even before his death. His story teaches us to cherish the sacrifices of Rizal and other national heroes who devoted their lives to gaining the country’s independence. As a student, I promise to honor the national hero not only by remembering his death but also by following his example of patriotism, education, and service to the nation.
