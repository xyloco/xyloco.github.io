José Rizal: Life & Works

About Our Project

A student-created educational website dedicated to exploring the life, works, experiences, and legacy of Dr. José Rizal.

Our Purpose

Making History Meaningful

Learning about the past helps us better understand the present and our responsibilities toward the future.

The José Rizal: Life & Works website was created to present the life and contributions of Dr. José Rizal in an organized and accessible format. It explores important stages of his life, including his childhood, education, travels, literary works, exile, and final journey.

Through historical information and supporting references, the project aims to help students and readers appreciate Rizal's ideas, his dedication to learning, and his role in Philippine history.

This website is a collaborative academic project. The team worked to organize the content and create a design that makes the material easier to navigate and understand.

Personal Reflection

Dr. José Rizal is a great example of a Filipino hero because he used his knowledge, talents, and courage to fight for the rights of his fellow Filipinos. He is the only one who didn't use any weapons just to become a hero back then. Instead of using weapons, he used his knowledge to fight and to expose the abuses and injustices that his fellow Filipinos experienced during the Spanish colonial period. He showed that we don’t actually need any weapons to fight for ourselves or for our freedom but words and education could also be powerful tools for change and justice that we need.

As a second-year college student, I learned from Dr. Jose Rizal that education should not only be used to achieve our personal success but we can also use our knowledge and ideas to understand the problems in our community and help our fellow Filipinos. His life reminds me that standing up for ourselves and for other people requires courage. Yes, it is difficult and it may put us in a very dangerous spot but we need to be brave and have the courage to risk everything for the betterment of our lives. Rizal's sacrifice also teaches me to appreciate the freedom and opportunities we have today. He dedicated much of his life to fighting for the dignity and welfare of Filipinos, even though his actions placed him in danger. Maybe without him and without our other brave heroes, we wouldn’t be where we are today, and we would still be under the rule of the Japanese or the other past colonizers.

His execution on December 30, 1896, reminds us of the great sacrifices made by people who fought for our country. I believe that we can honor Rizal by being responsible students, respecting other people, respecting our history, learning from our history, visiting historical places to give our respect and using our knowledge to help our community to improve. We do not have to become famous to make a difference and we don’t need to use any weapons just to fight for justice but we can use our knowledge to do it. For me, true kabayanihan begins with loving and respecting our country, and showing that love through our everyday actions.

Personal Reflection

Learning about the events of José Rizal’s life before he was killed was indeed an eye-opener for me, as I understood the sacrifices that he made for the country. Although Rizal believed that the time was ripe for revolution, he believed that an armed uprising should not begin without enough preparation and support. It was also fascinating to see his journey as a doctor and a patriot who did not abandon his duty as a physician even when his country was in dire need of a leader. Still, Rizal did not fail to disappoint as the events surrounding his arrest and the unfair trial that led to his execution revealed the injustice that he was subjected to by the Spanish authorities.

Perhaps the most intriguing part was observing the life of a patriot who had accepted death as a fate but continued to express his love for the Philippines, as evident in his poem Mi Último Adiós. Rizal, who realized that his time had come, made sure to signify the importance of freedom in his countrymen’s lives, even before his death. His story teaches us to cherish the sacrifices of Rizal and other national heroes who devoted their lives to gaining the country’s independence. As a student, I promise to honor the national hero not only by remembering his death but also by following his example of patriotism, education, and service to the nation.

Personal Reflection

After reading about the childhood of Dr. Jose Rizal, I learned that his early life played an important role in shaping him into a great Filipino hero. Even at a young age, Rizal showed that he was intelligent, curious, and eager to learn. His mother taught him the alphabet, reading, and writing, while his uncles helped him appreciate nature and understand the importance of education. These experiences taught me that the people around us can have an influence on our growth, becoming who we are in the future.

One thing that caught my attention was how Rizal experienced sadness at an early age when his younger sister Concepcion died. He also faced difficulties when his mother was imprisoned. Despite these problems and challenges in life, he continued his education and developed his talents in writing, language, and painting. This made me realize that life is not always easy, but the challenges we experience can teach us a lesson for life, it also taught us that we can stay stronger even when we have challenges in life.

I was also inspired by Rizal's love for our language. At the age of eight, he wrote his first poem, Sa Aking Mga Kabata, which focused on the love of one's language. This shows that even as a child, he already valued his identity, culture and love for the country. For me, this is a reminder that we should be proud of our own language, respect our culture, and appreciate our history as Filipinos.

Overall, learning about Rizal's childhood helped me understand that becoming a successful person in life begins with small steps, such as studying hard, being curious, and listening to the guidance of our family and teachers. I realized that we do not have to force ourselves to be great easily because we can develop our abilities through patience, effort, and determination. Rizal's childhood inspires me to value my education and use my knowledge and talents to become a great person someday, good daughter to my family and also to inspire others to study hard for the future and be who you are.
